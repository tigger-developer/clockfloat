## Scope

The pre-migration regression baseline requires persisted time and date font sizes of 0.014 and 0.01 to load as the established defaults of 14 and 10 points, respectively.

This criterion is bounded by the maintained test `test_load_tinyFontSize_resetsToDefault` and the defaults recorded in that test.
